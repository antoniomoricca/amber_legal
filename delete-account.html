Delete Your Ambry Account

App: Ambry  ·  Last updated: November 8, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Ambry account and associated personal data. These instructions are provided for App Store and Google Play requirements and for users who need a public reference.

1. Delete from inside the App (recommended)

  1. Open the Ambry app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Review the deletion preview (including any groups you solely own that will also be deleted).
  5. Type the confirmation word shown on screen, then confirm.

Deletion starts immediately. We complete erasure of your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Apple guideline 5.1.1(v): account deletion is available in-app under Profile → Delete account. You do not need to email us to delete an account you can still access.

2. If you cannot access the App

Email support@ambry-app.com from the address associated with your Ambry account, with subject line “Delete my Ambry account”. Include:

  • The email address used to register
  • Your display name, if you remember it
  • A brief confirmation that you want permanent deletion

We will verify ownership and process the request. We aim to confirm within a few business days and complete deletion within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your profile, email, and authentication records for Ambry
  • Your personal library (saves, notes, media you uploaded for yourself)
  • Your membership in shared groups; content you added to shared groups is removed from those groups
  • Groups you solely own may be deleted as part of the process
  • Optional location tags attached to your items
  • Push notification tokens associated with your account

4. What may remain

  • Content that other users independently saved in their own libraries
  • Limited records we must keep for legal, security, or accounting reasons (if any)
  • Anonymised technical logs that no longer identify you
