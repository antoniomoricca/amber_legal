Privacy Policy

App: Ambry  ·  Last updated: November 8, 2026  ·  Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Antonio Moricca ("we", "our", "us"), the developer of the Ambry mobile application ("App"), collects, uses, and protects the personal data of users ("you") in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and applicable French data protection law.

By creating an account or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

Official website and legal pages: https://www.ambry-app.com.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

Antonio Moricca
Support & privacy: support@ambry-app.com
Support page: https://www.ambry-app.com/support

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the data that is strictly necessary to provide the App's functionality.

Account data

  • Email address
  • Display name / nickname
  • Profile picture (optional, uploaded by you)

User-generated content

  • Photos, videos, audio, and media files you choose to upload or save within the App
  • Links and references to third-party content you manually select and save
  • Notes, tags, comments, reactions, and other metadata you attach to saved items
  • Content you share with groups you join or create (visible to other members of those groups)

Location data

  • If you choose to attach a location to a saved item, we request your device's location permission and may store the geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) and/or a place name or region label linked to that item.
  • Location is used only to organise and display your content on maps and location-based views that you opt into.
  • Location access is always optional. You can deny the OS permission and still use the App without location features. You can remove a location from an item at any time.
  • We do not use location for advertising, background tracking, or continuous monitoring.

Authentication data

  • We use Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) to manage sign-in. You may create an account with email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple.
  • Depending on the method you choose, Firebase and the identity provider may process identifiers such as your email, name, provider user ID, and device/security tokens needed to authenticate you.
  • See Firebase Privacy and the privacy policies of Google and Apple for provider-side processing.

Device and notification data

  • If you enable push notifications, we may store a device push token to deliver App-related notifications you consented to receive.
  • We may process basic technical logs (for example request timestamps and error diagnostics) needed to operate and secure the service.

Third-party SDKs and services we use

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) — account creation and sign-in (email/password, Google, Apple)
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method
  • Sign in with Apple — optional authentication method (required when Google Sign-In is offered on iOS)
  • Apple Push Notification service / Google FCM (via Expo Notifications) — only if you opt in to push notifications
  • Our own backend servers — storage of account data, content, group membership, and optional location tags

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, or other behavioural analytics / crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers for ads.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To store, organise, and display your saved content within the App
  • To enable optional group sharing with people you choose to collaborate with
  • To provide location-based organisation features when you opt in
  • To send you push notifications related to the App (only with your prior consent)
  • To respond to your support, privacy, and safety requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

The legal bases for processing under the GDPR are: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for core App functionality, and your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for optional features such as location tagging and push notifications.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Content

Your personal library is private by default. Content becomes visible to other Ambry users only when you explicitly share it in a group or publish a collection you choose to make available.

The App also allows you to manually save references to content from third-party platforms (such as links or embedded previews). When you save such content:

  • The original content remains hosted on and owned by the respective third-party platform and its creator.
  • Ambry displays the original source and author attribution where available.
  • Ambry provides a link back to the original content on the source platform where available.
  • You are solely responsible for ensuring your use of third-party content complies with the terms of service of the respective platform.

The App contains links to third-party services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on our servers hosted on a dedicated virtual machine managed by us. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Firebase Authentication infrastructure is operated by Google LLC and hosted within the European Economic Area where possible. For more information, see Firebase Privacy.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

You can delete your account at any time from the App (Profile → Delete account). Step-by-step instructions are also published at https://www.ambry-app.com/delete-account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and personal content. Content that other users independently kept in their own libraries may remain. Groups you solely own may also be deleted as part of account deletion.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a resident of the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@ambry-app.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. In France, this is the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Push Notifications

We request your explicit consent before sending push notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time via your device's notification settings or by contacting support.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
